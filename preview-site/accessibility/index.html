Inclusive access

Accessibility Statement

FixAds aims to make its multilingual marketing and legal pages usable by as many people as possible and uses WCAG 2.1 Level AA as its design and testing target.

Measures in the current website

  • Keyboard-accessible navigation, forms, links, and visible focus states.
  • A skip link and semantic headings and landmarks.
  • English and German left-to-right layouts and a fully mirrored Hebrew right-to-left layout.
  • Labels, validation feedback, and practical touch targets in contact forms.
  • Reduced or removed animation when the browser requests reduced motion.
  • Responsive layouts tested on narrow phone screens without horizontal scrolling.

Current status

The website is reviewed during development against the target above, but it has not been certified by an independent accessibility auditor. Third-party websites reached through external links and the separately protected client portal may have their own accessibility implementations.

Feedback and assistance
