# FixAds market refinement — 11 September 2026

## Research scope and limits

Reviewed public agency pages in Israel, Germany and the United States. This is a positioning/content comparison, not a measurement of conversion success. Rankings, traffic, backlinks, domain age, CITE scores and AI citation frequency are N/A: no verified analytics were available. Competitor claims are not FixAds claims. No wording, results, testimonials or partner badges were copied.

| Market / competitor | Observation | FixAds decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Israel — [SFB](https://www.sfb.co.il/) | Direct business outcomes, visible owner-led contact, practical buying questions. | Natural Hebrew focused on turning interest into customers; owner introduction; useful FAQs. |
| Germany — [Daily Rocket](https://dailyrocket.de/) | Specific service scope and an understandable initial conversation. | Define examples of the work; discuss scope and fees separately from media spend; avoid invented prices. |
| Germany — [morefire](https://www.more-fire.com/) | Connects services through an explicit customer journey and explains how the team works. | Show the inquiry-to-CRM-to-follow-up journey with four expandable steps; make responsibility clear. |
| United States — [Scorpion HVAC](https://www.scorpion.co/hvac/) | Industry-specific language connects marketing with calls and booked work. | HVAC/service panel emphasizes qualified inquiries and the follow-up process, not just lead volume. |
| United States — [KlientBoost](https://www.klientboost.com/) | Lets visitors identify the business outcome they need before reading detailed services. | Retain the existing three-goal selector and make its examples of work more concrete. |

These patterns are hypotheses for reducing uncertainty and improving relevance, not proof that this release will increase leads.

## Implementation and handoff

- Immediate / implemented: native localized hero copy and calls to action, two examples of work per goal, six FAQs, customer-care principles, a public owner introduction, a four-step integration example, three original visuals, relevant metadata.
- Short term / owner input needed: gather permission and evidence for two or three real client case studies (baseline, intervention, dates and verified results). No placeholder statistics or fake testimonials were added.
- Longer term / separate scope: assess actual inquiry quality and conversion with an approved privacy-aware measurement plan. No tracking or cookie/consent changes were silently introduced.
- Preserve: Transparency routes and bytes, form registration and notification settings, country access/routing policy, footer-only language control, German Impressum, real brand/client/platform logos.
- Validation: phone/desktop layouts, keyboard/native disclosures, Hebrew RTL, form transport behavior, lightweight motion, Lighthouse, then protected draft/live checks. Exact results are recorded in the root README Change Log.
- Handoff: content/UX changes implemented in the existing static renderer and content model. SEO or GEO traffic uplift is not claimed. Future content changes must update the living README and Change Log.
