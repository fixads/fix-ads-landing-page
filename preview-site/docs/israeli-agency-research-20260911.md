# Israeli agency research and Hebrew refinement — 11 September 2026

## Finding

FixAds needs direct, natural Hebrew that explains the business goal and the work. The useful comparison is how established agencies explain their offer, not whether FixAds can claim their scale. The three references below are agencies serving business/brand clients; this is not an audited ranking by revenue, headcount or media spend.

## Writing decisions

- Use an original, concise headline: **שיווק דיגיטלי. עם חשיבה עסקית.**
- Describe the customer's need: qualified inquiries, purchases, repeat customers, or less manual work.
- Use Hebrew for inquiry handling and ongoing follow-up; retain Google, Meta, Shopify, Klaviyo, CRM and Odoo where specific.
- Keep a consistent plural address: אתם / שלכם. Explain the next step without promising a deadline, free work or guaranteed results.
- Preserve the real client list, current services, international experience and customer-care positioning.
- Replace the old personal-review byline on the rewritten Hebrew service page with a neutral FixAds publisher/update line; do not claim a fresh human review.
