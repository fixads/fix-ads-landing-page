# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

FixAds serves business owners and marketing decision-makers in Israel, Germany, the United States, and other allowed international markets. Core audiences include e-commerce brands, HVAC and service businesses, professional-services firms, and other lead-driven organizations. Visitors need to understand whether FixAds can connect acquisition, websites, lifecycle marketing, automation, integrations, and measurement into one accountable growth system.

## Product Purpose

The website presents FixAds as a customer-focused digital growth partner that connects paid media, e-commerce, lead generation, websites, email, bots, automation, Odoo, platform integrations, and analytics. Success means a visitor quickly understands the complete service system, trusts the agency's transparent working style, and can confidently start a conversation or access the protected client interface.

## Brand Personality

Candid, inventive, accountable. The experience should feel energetic and technically capable without becoming cold, showy, or overcomplicated. Customer care and commercial practicality should remain visible beneath every technology claim.

## Anti-references

- Generic agency templates that could belong to any company.
- Unsupported performance promises, invented ratings, or platform-partner claims.
- Technology spectacle that obscures the customer problem or business outcome.
- Timid corporate styling, decorative white logo boxes, and inconsistent brand assets.
- Localization that treats Hebrew RTL or German market expectations as an afterthought.
- Motion that distracts, blocks content, or ignores reduced-motion preferences.

## Design Principles

1. Show the connected system: make relationships between marketing, technology, customer experience, and data understandable.
2. Earn trust through clarity: use specific, supportable language and keep client data transparency central.
3. Practice customer care: make navigation, contact, and reading effortless, especially on phones.
4. Localize the experience, not only the words: respect Hebrew RTL and the expectations of U.S., international, and German audiences.
5. Use energy with purpose: bold color, imagery, and motion should clarify momentum and capability rather than decorate empty space.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA behavior. Preserve semantic HTML, keyboard access, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, useful alternative text, reduced-motion support, 44–48px practical touch targets, phone-safe layouts, and correct `lang` and `dir` metadata. Hebrew RTL must be tested as a complete layout, and mixed-direction form values must remain readable.
